BEFORE WE BEGIN

A little
more clarity.

Which franchise model is right for me?

FOFO suits partners who want to operate their own outlet. FOCO is an agreed company-operated format. Hotel & Resort Spa brings wellness into an existing hospitality property. Our team can help you compare them.

What kind of space should I consider?

The indicative outlet size is 800–1,500 sq. ft. The final layout and space requirements depend on the selected model and location evaluation.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

Your enquiry goes to our team for review. We discuss your plans, arrange the next conversation or site visit, and work through a suitable proposal and agreement.
